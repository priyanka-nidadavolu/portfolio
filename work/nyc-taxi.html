NYC taxi pipeline

Data engineeringStatistics

Partition-parallel ETL and analysis over 10 years of NYC TLC trip data: 443 Parquet files, 57 GB, 3.4B+ rows, on one machine.

01The problem

One year of TLC trip data is around 10 GB compressed, and the full multi-year set is 57 GB of Parquet, far beyond what pandas can hold in memory. The usual options are crashing, paying for a cluster or downsampling and losing signal.

The goal was to process all of it on a single machine without approximation, then layer real analysis on top.

Key insightLazy evaluation is the whole trick: build the computation graph first, and only pay for what you actually compute.

02What I built

Distributed processing

Dask DataFrames replace pandas for out-of-core, parallel work. Nothing is read from disk until .compute().

Column pruning

Reading only the needed columns from Parquet gave a 3 to 4× speedup over reading everything and dropping later.

Data quality

Filtered impossible trips, capped fare outliers at the 99th percentile and handled schema drift across years.

Time-series analysis

Hourly, weekly and seasonal features with rolling averages for ride volume, fares and tip rates.

PCA on zones

Zone-level statistics reduced to 2D revealed airport, tourist and outer-borough clusters without any coordinates.

Modeling

Heavy-tail analysis, bootstrap stability checks, a GAM fare model and an XGBoost classifier.

04What the data showed

Time

Dual demand peaks

Weekday peaks at 8 to 9am and 5 to 7pm; weekends swap the commute spike for late-night demand.

Space

Three zone clusters

Airports (long trips, high fares), Midtown and tourist zones (short trips, high tips) and the outer boroughs.

Money

Card riders tip more

Card payments average an 18.2% tip versus 12.1% for cash, across every borough and time window.

Season

Winter dip

January and February run about 15% below summer demand.

05What I learned

  • Column pruning at read time is the cheapest big speedup there is.
  • PCA on aggregated zone statistics is surprisingly good at spatial segmentation.
  • Schema drift between years has to be validated before unioning partitions.
